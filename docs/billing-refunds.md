# Billing, charges and refunds

You can view every charge and download invoices under **Billing → Invoices** in your account settings.

If you were charged twice for the same period, the duplicate charge is refunded automatically once it settles; if both charges still show after 3 business days, contact support with the invoice numbers.

Refund policy: we offer a refund within 30 days of a charge if the service was not used. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. To request a refund, reply to your invoice email or open a billing ticket with the invoice number.
