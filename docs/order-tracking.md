# Tracking your order and delivery

Every order gets a tracking link in the shipping confirmation email, and the same tracking information is on the **Orders** page in your account.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first update after the shipping label is created. If your package shows no movement for more than 3 business days, or the delivery date has passed and the order hasn't arrived, contact support with your order number and we will follow up with the carrier.

Delivery estimates shown at checkout are business-day estimates and can shift during peak periods.
