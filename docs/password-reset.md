# Resetting your password

If you forgot your password or are locked out and can't log in, you can reset it yourself:

1. On the sign in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account and submit the form.
3. Open the password reset email and choose **Reset password**. The link expires after 30 minutes.
4. Or, if you are already signed in, go to **Settings → Security → Reset password**.

If the reset email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the email address linked to your account. After resetting, sign in with your new password on all your devices.
