# Troubleshooting app errors and crashes

If the app keeps crashing, freezes, or shows an error message, work through these steps in order:

1. **Update the app** to the latest version from your app store.
2. **Clear the app cache** (Settings → Apps → clear cache) and restart the app.
3. Sign out, restart your device, and sign back in.
4. If the error shows a code, note the exact error code and the screen where it appears.
5. As a last step, uninstall and reinstall the app; your data is stored in your account, not on the device.

If the crash continues after a reinstall, open a ticket with the error code, your device model and what you were doing when the app stopped working.
