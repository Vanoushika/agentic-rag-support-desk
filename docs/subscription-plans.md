# Subscription plans: upgrading, downgrading and cancelling

You can change your subscription plan at any time under **Billing → Subscription**.

- **Upgrade:** takes effect immediately, and you are charged the prorated difference for the rest of the current cycle.
- **Downgrade:** takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, so you keep your current plan's features until then.
- **Cancel:** you can cancel your subscription at any time; access continues until the end of the paid period and you will not be charged again.

If your plan change doesn't show after an hour, sign out and back in, then check **Billing → Subscription** again.
